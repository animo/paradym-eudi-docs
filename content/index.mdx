# Welcome

<Callout type="important">Questions or issues? Read about our weekly open call or how to contact us [here](contact.mdx).</Callout>

This is your guide to the Paradym EUDI Wallet 🇪🇺. 
 
The Paradym Wallet is an open-source identity wallet. You can use the wallet, and its codebase, in any way that is helpful to you. This site describes how to integrate with and use the [Public Beta](/install-the-wallet) of the Paradym Wallet, which supports experimental features and is used in several EU Pilots.

For the public, stable, version of the Paradym Wallet you can download the app for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/nl/app/paradym-wallet/id6449846111?l=en) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=id.paradym.wallet). 

The wallet is a mobile companion to the [Paradym](https://paradym.id/) digital identity platform, which handles issuance, verification and trust management for Relying Parties.
