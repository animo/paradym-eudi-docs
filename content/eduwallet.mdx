# Eduwallet

The Eduwallet is a Dutch project started by [SURF](https://www.surf.nl/en) and [NPuls](https://npuls.nl/en) to explore the use of the EU Digital Identity Wallet for Education use cases. Through the Eduwallet project they are investigating how a digital wallet enables learners to independently store and share their results and credentials, even after graduation. 

Paradym is partnering with the [University of Applied Sciences Amsterdam](https://www.hva.nl/) to issue SD-JWT VC Diploma Supplements, and the Paradym Wallet will be interoperable with all solutions and partners participating in the project. 

Testing environment: https://eduwallet.nl/en
